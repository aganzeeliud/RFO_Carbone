Calculation Methodology

Avoided Emissions Formula: Avoided_GHG = (ΔLoss × Biomass Density × 0.47) × 3.67

  • ΔLoss: Difference between Business-As-Usual deforestation rate and actual reserve loss
  • 0.47: IPCC conversion factor (carbon content of dry biomass)
  • 3.67: Molecular conversion factor (C → CO2)
  • Reference Period: 2001-2026 (25 years of continuous monitoring)
