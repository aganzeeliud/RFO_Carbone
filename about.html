About the Project

Understanding the role of the Okapi Wildlife Reserve in global climate mitigation.

The Okapi Wildlife Reserve (RFO)

Occupying about one-fifth of the Ituri Forest in the north-east of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the Okapi Wildlife Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage site of immense biological and carbon value. It contains the largest population of Okapis in the wild and provides a critical carbon sink for the planet.

Significance of the Congo Basin

The Congo Basin is the "second lung" of the world. Unlike the Amazon, which has become a net carbon emitter in some areas, the Congo Basin remains a robust carbon sink. Protecting the Okapi Reserve is not just about biodiversity—it is a global climate necessity.
