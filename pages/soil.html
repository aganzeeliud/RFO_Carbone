Forest Stratification

The reserve is dominated by **Dense Humid Forest**, but includes significant areas of **Swamp Forest** along river basins and **Secondary Forest** in recovered areas.

  • Dense Humid High carbon density (165.5 tC/ha)
  • Swamp Forest Medium carbon density (142.2 tC/ha)
  • Secondary Regenerating stock (120.8 tC/ha)
