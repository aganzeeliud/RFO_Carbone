Scientific Methodology

A multi-sensor approach to forest carbon and biomass monitoring.

1. Data Acquisition

We leverage Google Earth Engine to aggregate multi-temporal satellite imagery, including Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2, combined with NASA GEDI (Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation) Lidar data.

2. Carbon Modeling

Aboveground Biomass (AGB) is converted to Carbon using the standard fraction of 0.47. Sequestration is calculated by subtracting annual emissions from forest loss from the total incremental growth.

3. Verification

Results are cross-referenced with ESA Biomass CCI products and UN Biodiversity Lab datasets to ensure high-fidelity reporting suitable for REDD+ and UNESCO standards.

The Carbon Equation

Net Carbon Balance = (Forest Area * ΔBiomass * 0.47) - (Lost Area * Baseline Biomass * 0.47)

Our workflow accounts for both **deforestation** (complete conversion) and **degradation** (partial loss of canopy cover), which is often overlooked in traditional forest monitoring but critical in the Congo Basin due to artisanal mining impacts.
