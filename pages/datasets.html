Lidar

NASA GEDI L4A

High-resolution Aboveground Biomass Density (AGBD) derived from spaceborne LiDAR waveforms at 25m footprint resolution.

  • Timeline: 2019 - 2023
  • Product: Footprint AGBD
  • Provider: NASA LP DAAC

Optical

Global Forest Watch

Hansen Global Forest Change dataset monitoring annual tree cover loss and gain using Landsat multi-decadal archives.

  • Timeline: 2000 - 2024
  • Resolution: 30m
  • Provider: UMD / GFW
Explore GFW
Radar

ESA Biomass CCI

European Space Agency's globally consistent biomass maps utilizing multi-frequency SAR and optical fusion.

  • Timeline: 2010, 2017 - 2022
  • Resolution: 100m
  • Provider: ESA Climate Office
