Total Carbon Stock (GtC)

Source: IPCC 2006, Global Forest Watch.

Resume: Carbon storage in the Congo Basin is critical for global climate stability. The Okapi Reserve protects one of the highest biomass density zones within the basin, acting as a stable long-term reservoir.
