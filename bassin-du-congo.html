Bassin du Congo

Comparative analysis of the Congo Basin's carbon dynamics and the Okapi Wildlife Reserve's strategic contribution (2000-2026).

Carbon Sequestration (MtCO2e/yr)

Source: NASA GEDI, ESA Biomass CCI, UNESCO.

Resume: The Congo Basin remains the world's strongest tropical carbon sink. While regional trends show slight fluctuations due to agricultural expansion, the Okapi Reserve maintains an upward trajectory in sequestration efficiency.

Total Carbon Stock (GtC)

Source: IPCC 2006, Global Forest Watch.

Resume: Carbon storage in the Congo Basin is critical for global climate stability. The Okapi Reserve protects one of the highest biomass density zones within the basin, acting as a stable long-term reservoir.

Avoided GHG Emissions (tCO2e)

Source: Verra VM0015, GFW Alerts.

Resume: Conservation efforts in Okapi significantly outperform regional averages in preventing deforestation-linked emissions, contributing disproportionately to the basin's total mitigation.
